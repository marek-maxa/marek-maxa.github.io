## Context

This project began as a personal portfolio, but the more interesting requirement was editorial rather than visual. The site needed to stay simple on the surface while still being able to hold longer project notes, formulas, and richer explanations over time.

> [!NOTE]
> The long-term goal is not to build a flashy portfolio, but a small system that makes technical writing easier to publish and easier to read.

## Design direction

The current direction is intentionally restrained. Pages are narrower, the project index is simpler, and the detailed project pages behave more like short articles than product cards.

> [!IDEA]
> A useful next step would be small side assets such as diagrams, screenshots, or short visual comparisons that support the text without overwhelming it.
