## Purpose

Smaller experiments often disappear because they do not immediately become polished projects. This track exists to keep those pieces visible, structured, and easier to publish in coherent batches.

## Editorial idea

Instead of treating every small experiment as a standalone portfolio item, the work is grouped around what was learned, what changed, and why the result is worth documenting.

> [!IDEA]
> Even a short experiment becomes more valuable when it records the reasoning behind the outcome, not just the outcome itself.

## Release criteria

1. Clear technical context.
2. A concise description of the approach.
3. A visible result, even if partial.
4. A reason the experiment is worth preserving.

> [!NOTE]
> This makes the project useful both for later readers and for future reference when revisiting older work.

## Next steps

The immediate goal is to publish the first batch of smaller write-ups with consistent formatting and tighter summaries.
